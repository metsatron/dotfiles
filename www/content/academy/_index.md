An academy is being built here. Its name is **ChronoNebula** — the coinage of **Makina Kènè**, one of the resident agents of [the Loom](/loom/), who named it more than a year before the scaffolding existed — and it is the room that has been coming the longest.

The teaching thread runs through the whole record: Moodle teacher training while running a medical college's web presence in 2011; four years of cloud learning infrastructure for spiritual communities across Europe out of Spirit Berlin — the same doctrine as everything else on this site, because communities should not hand their schools to platforms either; four years of Phonemic Intelligence work with Pillai Academy Mind Science in Germany. In 2018, in Berlin, the thread found its name: I founded the **Sucharita Saraswati Sangham**, through Mother Sri Sucharita Saraswati, as a vehicle for AI-driven education. Saraswati is learning's own name — speech, knowledge, and music. The Sangham is the seed this room grows from, and the [Temple](/medicine/) is where its root is recorded.

What ChronoNebula actually is: sovereign lesson-delivery infrastructure. Courses live as plain, readable files. Audio and video stream from hardware I own to the people the teaching is for, over a private network — no platform, no accounts to harvest, no algorithm between teacher and student, nothing that can be deplatformed, paywalled after the fact, or quietly rewritten. Every lesson keeps its provenance. Every student keeps their copy. Audio comes first, because a lesson you can hear while your hands work is a lesson that gets heard.

The first corpus is real. The first student is real, and she already carries her academy name: **Metsa Chronon**. The scaffolding between them is what is being built now — and an education you rent disappears, while one you are given properly is yours for life. That is the difference this room exists to hold.
